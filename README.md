# THE WORLD TODAY — Sunday 22 November 2026

A photo-essay front page summing up the world's news of Sunday 22 November
2026 in one image. Three large illustrated hero panels lead the page — a
tanker on fire in the Strait of Hormuz, a cracked COP31 globe, the Capitol
dome over the Democrats' 258 seats — followed by six captioned photo frames,
a pinned map, and the day in brief (stories 10–32).

## How we know

- **REPORTED** — confirmed by 2+ sources.
- **CLAIM** — one side alleges, unverified.
- **ONGOING** — still developing.

Date check: every story above was first reported on 22 Nov, unless its

## Why a photo essay

A picture editor's page, not a wall of text: each big story gets a picture
first — the burning tanker, the cracked globe, the dome — so a reader who
missed the day can tell what happened from the images alone, with the
captions and the numbered brief carrying the detail. The six frames extend
the same idea down the page: every story you see, you also see.

## How to run

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`) and verifies its size and
SHA-256, so two runs write identical bytes. `draw.py` is the PIL program
that drew the front page.
